## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an environment of restorative calm, deliberate warmth, and quietly elevated clinical excellence. Designed for dental patients—many of whom carry deep-seated anxiety and sensory vulnerability—the visual atmosphere consciously rejects cold, hyper-sterile whites, harsh fluorescent contrasts, and patronizing cartoon metaphors. Instead, it balances the composure of an upscale hospitality sanctuary with the rigorous precision of modern, gentle dentistry.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
The style merges **Organic Warm Minimalism** with **Refined Tactile Editorial** accents:
- **Atmospheric Reassurance:** Expansive, breathing layouts that feel unhurried, peaceful, and open.
- **Human Tactility:** Natural paper tones, gentle porcelain ceramic finishes, and brushed brass architectural accents.
- **Poised Dignity:** Sophisticated optical typography paired with an approachable, humanist structure, reassuring patients of both consummate medical mastery and empathetic care.

## Colors

The palette is tuned to lower physiological stress, drawing inspiration from natural linen, porcelain ceramics, apothecary herbs, and subtle jeweler-grade brass.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1B6B6F` - Refined Deep Medical Teal):** Conveys stability, clinical competence, and oxygenating clarity. Used for primary actions, critical interactive states, and anchored brand elements.
- **Secondary (`#A9C0B3` - Serene Sage Green):** Evokes healing, botanical calmness, and biological renewal. Serves as soft wash backgrounds, secondary containers, and calming badge fills.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#B8935A` - Warm Brushed Brass):** Provides artisanal dignity. Expressed strictly as fine framing hairlines, subtle active pips, decorative monograms, and delicate ornamental dividers.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#0F2E33` - Deep Ink-Teal):** Replaces harsh jet black with a deep, marine-steeped organic pigment. Delivers WCAG AAA contrast against linen surfaces without sensory shock.
- **Base Canvas:** The default background is warm linen (`#F6F1E8`), while elevated surfaces adopt soft porcelain white (`#FCFAF6`).

### Contrast & Usability Guidelines
- All text combinations must surpass WCAG AA (4.5:1 for normal body text, 3:1 for large display). Ink-teal on porcelain achieves 11.2:1; on linen it achieves 9.8:1.
- Brass hairline accents (`#B8935A`) must never be used for raw body text; they are strictly reserved for non-text structural lines and decorative highlights at 30% to 100% opacity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the soft, bookish elegance of an optical serif with an impeccably clean, legible geometric humanist sans.

### Font Roles
- **Headings & Display (Newsreader):** Used for all headlines, pull-quotes, and doctor titles. Features generous x-height, organic terminal teardrops, and warm curves that immediately dismantle clinical anxiety.
- **Body & Supporting UI (Manrope):** Clean, spacious, and highly legible. The minimum scale for patient-facing body text is set to 16px to ensure effortless reading for all age groups and under various lighting conditions.
- **Eyebrows & Microcopy (Manrope All-Caps):** Used sparingly for category tags, department labels, and process steps. Rendered in uppercase with an ultra-wide tracking rhythm (`letter-spacing: 0.14em`) to act as architectural signposts without creating visual tension.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an **8px baseline grid** governed by an intentional sense of "unhurried space." Negative space is treated as an active therapeutic agent rather than void space.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid. Outer margins: `margin` (3rem/48px). Gutters: `gutter` (1.5rem/24px). Maximum content container: 1200px to maintain serene line lengths (60–75 characters per line).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid. Outer margins: 2rem (32px). Gutters: 1.25rem (20px).
- **Mobile (&lt;768px):** 4-column fluid grid. Outer margins: `margin-mobile` (1.25rem/20px). Gutters: `gutter-mobile` (1rem/16px).

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical page sections must use comfortable breathing intervals (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to 5rem on desktop viewports) to avoid dense clustering.
- Interactive tap zones conform to a minimum dimension of 48×48px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through gentle tonal elevation and light-diffused atmosphere rather than pronounced drops, heavy outlines, or harsh contrast cutoffs.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Flat linen background (`#F6F1E8`), grounding the entire experience in warmth.
- **Resting Layer (Surface Cards):** `#FCFAF6` resting over `#F6F1E8`. Separated by a whisper-soft ambient multi-layered shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 46, 51, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 46, 51, 0.02)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Bars:** Layered surfaces employ a subtle backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(252, 250, 246, 0.88)` opacity to maintain spatial orientation without visual heaviness.
- **Architectural Framing:** Clean 1px brass hairline borders using `rgba(184, 147, 90, 0.30)` outline card contours and section separators, providing delicate structure reminiscent of fine Japanese wood-and-paper joinery.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is organic, sheltering, and ergonomic. Harsh 90-degree corners are eliminated to evoke reassurance and natural harmony.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Large Content Containers & Cards:** Molded with an ultra-soft `28px` corner radius (`rounded-[28px]`). This curvaceous envelope creates a soothing, sanctuary-like visual frame.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** Sculpted into complete pills (`rounded-full` / Pill-shaped). These provide intuitive, thumb-friendly touch targets that feel soft and inviting.
- **Dividers & Hairlines:** Crisp 1px strokes with a 30% brass tint (`border-[#B8935A]/30`), balancing soft shapes with structural poise.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), min-height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg` (24px). Background is deep medical teal (`#1B6B6F`), text is crisp porcelain white (`#FCFAF6`), typography is `label-button`. Subtle hover interaction: transforms with a -2px vertical lift and shadow expansion `0 8px 20px -2px rgba(27, 107, 111, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary / Calm Action:** Pill-shaped, porcelain white background (`#FCFAF6`) with a 1px brass hairline border (`rgba(184, 147, 90, 0.40)`). Text in deep ink-teal (`#0F2E33`). On hover, background shifts softly to `#F6F1E8`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Deep ink-teal text with an underline offset of 6px, accompanied by a directional monoline arrow.

### 2. Cards
- **Patient Journey & Service Cards:** Porcelain white fill (`#FCFAF6`), 28px border radius, 1px perimeter border in `rgba(184, 147, 90, 0.25)`. Internal padding of 24px (mobile) to 32px (desktop). Incorporates the signature ambient shadow `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 46, 51, 0.05)`.

### 3. Form Input Fields
- Minimum height 52px. Pill-shaped or rounded-2xl (16px), background in `#FCFAF6`, border 1px `rgba(15, 46, 51, 0.12)`. Text in ink-teal `#0F2E33`, placeholder in `rgba(15, 46, 51, 0.45)`.
- **Focus State:** 1.5px border in `#1B6B6F` with an exterior diffuse ring `0 0 0 4px rgba(27, 107, 111, 0.12)`. No abrupt transitions.

### 4. Chips & Filter Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 36px, padding 0 16px. Typography: `label-caption` (weight 500).
- **Default:** Background `rgba(169, 192, 179, 0.25)` (soft sage wash), text `#0F2E33`.
- **Selected:** Background `#1B6B6F`, text `#FCFAF6`.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom 22px circles/soft squares. Unchecked: 1.5px border in `rgba(184, 147, 90, 0.50)` over `#FCFAF6`. Checked: Filled with `#1B6B6F`, displaying an inner brass or porcelain tick/dot.

### 6. Iconography
- Consistent **1.5px stroke weight**, monoline construction, with rounded joints and ends. Never heavy, solid, or visually aggressive. Rendered in teal `#1B6B6F` or brass `#B8935A`.

### 7. Mobile Sticky Action Bar
- Affixed directly to the mobile viewport base with safe-area padding. Translucent porcelain background (`rgba(252, 250, 246, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), framed on top by a single 1px brass hairline (`rgba(184, 147, 90, 0.30)`).
- Houses four persistent, ergonomic touch targets:
  1. **Call Clinic:** Direct telephonic connection.
  2. **WhatsApp:** Instant discreet chat for anxious inquiries.
  3. **Directions:** Clinic navigation and parking guidance.
  4. **Book Appointment:** Highlighted primary pill button (`#1B6B6F`) for seamless reservation.
